# Asset and data credits

- **Construction sample image:** A raw construction PPE frame from the [LibreYOLO construction-safety-gsnvb dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LibreYOLO/construction-safety-gsnvb), published in [IlliaSator/industrial-safety-vision](https://github.com/IlliaSator/industrial-safety-vision/blob/feature/industrial-safety-vision/docs/assets/dataset_ppe_example_01.jpg). Source dataset license: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The frame was not altered before inclusion.
- **Construction sample video:** [“Building construction Moira Close Broadwater Farm Haringey 2025 16”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Building_construction_Moira_Close_Broadwater_Farm_Haringey_2025_16.webm) by **Acabashi**, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The included 34-second MP4 is a cut and conversion of that footage prepared by [Ksamaani/ppe-compliance-monitor](https://github.com/Ksamaani/ppe-compliance-monitor/blob/main/data/videos/site_clip.mp4). The source video is raw site footage, without detections painted onto it.
- **Training data:** [PyImageSearch construction-safety-object-detection-paligemma](https://huggingface.co/datasets/pyimagesearch/construction-safety-object-detection-paligemma), derived from construction-safety object-detection material. Review the source dataset's access terms before reusing its files; this repository includes the executed notebook and resulting model, not the training images.
- **Browser inference runtime:** [ONNX Runtime Web 1.31.1](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE). The required JavaScript and WebAssembly files are included in `site/vendor/`.
