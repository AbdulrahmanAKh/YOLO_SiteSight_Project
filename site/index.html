What happens inside?

The moving diagram shows inference: applying patterns learned during training to a new scene.

1 / Prepare

A scene becomes pixels

The image, or each video frame, is resized to fit 640 × 640 pixels. Padding keeps the original proportions.

2 / Detect

Learned patterns find objects

YOLO11n predicts object locations and 17 construction classes. Low confidence predictions and overlapping duplicates are filtered out.

3 / Review

Boxes become evidence

Boxes and confidence scores appear on your scene. Explicit no-hardhat, no-mask, and no-safety vest predictions are flagged for your review.

Trained for the construction site.

We fine-tuned the compact, pretrained YOLO11n detector for 40 epochs on the construction safety object detection dataset from PyImageSearch. It uses bounding-box annotations for workers, PPE, vehicles, and site equipment.

The notebook uses 307 training images, 57 validation images, and 34 held-out test images. This is object detection; the model draws boxes around objects.
